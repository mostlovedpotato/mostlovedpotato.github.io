Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who operates WorkBud

WorkBud is operated by Prashant Borkar as an individual. Primo is the public developer name. For privacy questions or deletion help, email infinitymathelon@gmail.com.

Information stored on your phone

Your optional private profile draft and PDF resume are stored in WorkBud's private app storage on your device. The app does not upload the PDF resume. These local files are not backed up by WorkBud. Removing app storage or uninstalling the app can permanently remove them. An encrypted sign-in refresh credential may be stored on the device; your password is not saved.

Information sent to WorkBud's service

When you use the shared service, we process your email and authentication record; business name; job title, area, description, schedule and pay; the name and skills you review and submit with an application; application and hiring status; and information in reports and blocks. We use this information to sign you in, show jobs, coordinate applications and offers, support account safety, investigate reports and respond to support requests.

WorkBud does not collect device GPS location, process wages or payments, or remotely store or share resume PDFs. Some demo content is fictional and remains on your device. Shared jobs and applications require an internet connection.

Who can see information

Signed-in users can see open job listings and business names. A business can review applications to its own jobs, including the name and skills a worker chose to submit. Workers can see their own application history. Reports are available to the person who submitted them and authorized operators; reported users cannot see the reporter's details. Supabase and other infrastructure or email service providers process information as needed to operate the service. We do not sell personal information or use advertising or analytics SDKs in this version.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and shared job/application records while needed to provide WorkBud, maintain hiring history, respond to reports, prevent abuse or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request account deletion in the app or at our account deletion page. When deletion completes, we remove the account and associated active WorkBud records as described on that page. A business account deletion also removes its jobs and dependent applications. If a technical failure prevents completion, access is disabled and you can contact support to finish or retry the request.

WorkBud does not keep a separate user-file archive. Supabase may retain operational logs or provider-managed backup copies under the current project plan and service terms; those copies may persist for a limited period after live records are deleted. The exact provider retention depends on the plan and settings in use and can change. Contact us for the current project retention details. Local drafts and resumes are removed only from the device where you delete them or clear app storage.

Your choices and safety

You can review the name and skills before submitting an application, remove local drafts or resumes, withdraw a pending application, block accounts, report a job or candidate, and request account deletion. Withdrawing an application does not erase a card already submitted to a business. Do not put passwords, identity documents, bank details or other sensitive information in job or application text.

WorkBud is intended for adults seeking or offering lawful work. We do not knowingly design the service for children.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if WorkBud's data practices change. The app may ask you to review updated terms before continuing to use shared features. For questions or privacy requests, email infinitymathelon@gmail.com.
