What WorkBud does

WorkBud is an early-stage service for adults to find local short-term work and for approved businesses to post shifts and review applicants. Business access is approved by the operator. Each posted shift is intended for one worker.

WorkBud provides the app and hiring coordination tools. It does not employ workers, guarantee that a listing is genuine or safe, guarantee that a shift will be filled or attended, or collect or pay wages. The worker and business must independently confirm the duties, work location, hours, pay amount, payment timing, required qualifications, safety arrangements and any applicable employment terms before agreeing to work. Applicable law determines each party's rights and obligations.

Accounts and acceptable use

Use accurate account and job information and keep your sign-in credentials private. Do not impersonate another person or business, post unlawful or misleading work, charge workers a recruitment fee through WorkBud, harass or threaten others, discriminate unlawfully, or share someone else's private information. Do not submit passwords, identity documents or bank details in job or application text.

Businesses must describe the work, pay, schedule, area and material requirements honestly. Workers should review a job and the application information they choose to share before applying.

Fees, cancellation and disputes

WorkBud currently charges no fee to workers or businesses and does not process payments. We may introduce a fee or paid feature only after clearly communicating the change and any required user choice. A fee is not owed under this version of the service.

If someone cannot attend or provide a shift, they should contact the other party directly as soon as possible and contact WorkBud support if help is needed. WorkBud does not decide wage or employment disputes. Users remain responsible for resolving arrangements between themselves and for seeking appropriate local assistance.

Reports, blocks and enforcement

Use the in-app report and block tools for suspicious jobs or conduct. The operator may review reports, remove content or restrict accounts to protect users and the service. Contact support to ask about an enforcement action or submit an appeal. Blocking hides relevant listings or candidate cards and prevents new interactions; it does not cancel an accepted shift. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Account deletion and service changes

You can request account deletion in the app or through the account deletion page. Deletion removes associated WorkBud records as described in the Privacy Policy. Coordinate any accepted work before deleting your account. Deletion does not settle an independent payment or work dispute. Optional resume PDFs and private drafts stay on your device and may need to be removed there separately.

We may update these terms when the service or its rules change. We will show the current version in the app; you may need to accept updated terms to continue using shared features. These terms do not limit rights that cannot legally be limited.
